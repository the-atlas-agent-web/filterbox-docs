FilterBox recovery

Reinstall your FilterBox

Reinstalling writes a fresh system image to the SD card. Everything on the box is replaced — settings, statistics and custom lists. Only do this when the box cannot recover on its own.

When a reinstall is the right move

  1. The box does not boot (no light, no Wi-Fi, no filterbox.lan after 10 minutes).
  2. A major system update is required and cannot be delivered automatically.
  3. A major Pi-hole update is required that the updater cannot install safely.
  4. The support team asks you to reinstall.

Small fixes — filtering, dashboard, languages — arrive automatically. You do not need a card reader for those.

1. Download the image

Latest system image (about 380 MB, compressed):

Download the latest image

The download is a .img.xz file. Do not unpack it — the flasher does that.

2. Write it to the SD card

macOS or Linux — with our flasher (checks the write afterwards):

  1. Insert the card and find its disk (diskutil list external on macOS, lsblk on Linux).
  2. Run flash-card.sh ~/Downloads/filterbox-*.img.xz --disk /dev/rdiskN
  3. Wait for FLASH VERIFIED OK, then eject.

Windows — use balenaEtcher or Rufus: choose the .img.xz file, choose the card, flash, verify.

3. Start it

  1. Put the card back in the box, connect the network cable where the router was, power on.
  2. Wait about three minutes.
  3. Open http://filterbox.lan on any phone or computer on the network.
  4. If the page does not open, try http://filterbox.local.

Reading this page on the box that needs reinstalling? Download the image to a computer first — the box cannot write to its own card.
